Why PrintNinja

Printing stops being a task. It becomes a button.

If your team prints the same receipts, labels and slips again and again, every extra click and every silent failure costs real time. PrintNinja removes both.

Printing todayBefore

  • A print dialog on every job — pick printer, paper, scale, again.
  • Thermal printers need the right driver, and the wrong one prints pages of gibberish.
  • Labels are laid out by trial and error, and the paper never matches the screen.
  • When nothing comes out, nobody knows if it is paper, power, or the network.
  • Reprints mean rebuilding the job from scratch.

With PrintNinjaAfter

  • One click sends the job straight to the printer. No dialogs.
  • Speaks the printer's own language — ESC/POS, ZPL, TSPL, CPCL — so no driver headaches.
  • Design in millimetres. The preview runs through the real print path, so it matches the paper.
  • A clear reason for every failure — out of paper, offline, wrong language.
  • Every job is kept in History, with one-tap reprint or retry.

How it works

One pipeline, from data to paper

Every entry point — the Print page, the local API, a deep link or a reprint — runs through the same pipeline, so what you preview is what you get.

  1. Data + templatesample, link or CSV
  2. Design pagesas in the Designer
  3. Sheetson physical paper
  4. Image or PDFrendered once
  5. Printer languageESC/POS · ZPL · TSPL · CPCL
  6. Wirenetwork · USB · Bluetooth
  7. Paperdone
  1. Add a printerNetwork, system, USB or Bluetooth. Press Test Print to confirm it is connected.
  2. Make a templatePick a starter — shipping label, receipt, invoice — and adjust it in the Designer.
  3. PrintChoose printer and template, add your data, check the preview, press Print.
  4. Check HistorySee a clear success or failure for each job. Reprint or retry in one tap.
Automation

Let your till, script or web page print for you

PrintNinja runs a local REST API on your PC, so any system that can send an HTTP request can print through the same pipeline as the app.

  • Safe retriesSend the same Idempotency-Key again and it never prints a second copy.
  • Real outcomes, plus callbacksThe response says whether it printed or why not, and an optional HTTPS callback reports when the job finishes.
  • Deep linksA link or button on a web page can start a print. Untrusted sources ask for confirmation first.
  • ObservableHealth checks, JSON and Prometheus metrics, and a live OpenAPI document.
POST /v1/print127.0.0.1:17717
# Print two copies of a receipt
curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:17717/v1/print \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: order-1042" \
  -d '{
    "template": "Receipt",
    "printer":  "Front Desk",
    "data": { "order": { "id": "1042", "total": 18.5 } },
    "settings": { "copies": 2 }
  }'
→ printed · GET /v1/jobs/{id} to track
Phone printing

Print templates from your phone — a PC steps in only when it has to

Most jobs run on the Android phone alone. The one step a phone cannot take is turning a template into a PDF, so that is the only time it asks a paired PC for help.

Which route does your job take?

The app checks which language the printer reads, then picks the route for you.

You press Print on the phone The app looks at the printer's language
No PC needed Receipt or label job template + your data
The phone renders it ESC/POS · ZPL · TSPL · CPCL · raster
Thermal printer Bluetooth · USB
Paired PC needed Template → PDF the one step a phone cannot take
A paired PC renders it Windows or Linux, with a browser engine
PDF-language printer office printers via network or system queue

What the paired PC actually does

Only one job is lent out: rendering. Everything else stays on the phone.

  1. Phone Lays out the pages template + data → sheets
  2. Phone → PC Sends the finished page with paper size, orientation, printer language and resolution
  3. PC Renders it with its own engine
  4. PC → Phone Returns finished bytes no side effects, safe to repeat
  5. Phone Prints and records History History notes which PC rendered it

Two ways to use a paired PC

Shared rendering

Phone→ PC renders→ Phone's printer

The PC prepares the page and the phone prints it on its own printer.

Print via PC (Android)

Phone→ PC renders and prints→ PC's printer

The whole job runs on the PC's printers with the PC's templates. If the phone's template is out of date you are asked to Sync — a wrong version is never printed.

Why it works this way

Each design choice has a reason, and none of them is a workaround.

Rendering needs a browser engine

Turning HTML and CSS into a finished PDF takes a full browser engine. Windows and Linux PCs have one; phones and macOS cannot render, so they are never chosen as the PC.

Thermal printers don't read PDF

Receipt and label printers speak command languages — ESC/POS, ZPL, TSPL, CPCL. The phone converts the layout into those itself, so no PDF and no PC are involved.

Rendering is the heavy part

Producing the image or PDF is by far the most expensive step of a print. That step goes to a PC with a proper engine; everything else stays on the phone.

Your prints stay on your network

Pairing is a deliberate step on the same Wi-Fi with a short-lived code, and traffic between phone and PC is encrypted. The cloud share service has no rendering engine, so a print never goes through it.

The phone stays in charge

A phone asks a PC, never the other way round. In shared rendering the PC never touches the phone's printers, data or History; the phone sends the bytes and records the job.

No silent failures

If the PC is off, asleep or away, the job fails at once and names the PC. Nothing waits silently in a queue and nothing falls back to another printer — retry from History when the PC is back.

Good to know: while pairing, the phone trusts the first certificate it sees, so pair on a network you trust.
