Printing that just works.

Pick a printer, pick a layout, press Print. No print dialogs, no driver headaches — receipts, labels and documents go straight to paper.

One path from data to paper

Every entry point — the Print page, the local API, a deep link or a reprint — uses the same pipeline. Design pages are finished before they are placed on physical paper, so the preview always matches the printout.

Two kinds of templates

Both live on the Templates page, and each row says which kind it is. The app refuses a wrong pairing before a byte is wasted.

Label templates

For thermal printers. Design on a canvas in millimetres: text, barcodes, QR codes, images, lines and frames. Printed in the printer's own language — ESC/POS, ZPL, TSPL or CPCL.

Document templates

For office and home printers. HTML and CSS with {{placeholders}} where your data goes. Start from Receipt, Invoice, Shipping Label and more.

Phone and PC, together

An Android phone prints to Bluetooth or USB receipt printers on its own. Only PDF work needs a paired PC on the same Wi-Fi — the phone asks, the PC renders and takes back the finished bytes.

Start in five minutes

  1. Add a printer. Network, system, USB and Bluetooth printers all work; press Test Print to be sure.
  2. Make a template. Pick a label starter or a ready-made document example, then arrange it in the Designer.
  3. Print. Choose printer and template, add sample data, a link or a CSV, check the preview and press Print.
  4. Check History. Every job records its real outcome, with one-tap reprint or retry.

Also useful

Automate it

A local REST API, deep links and placeholders let a till, script or web page print through the same pipeline.

Honest answers

No silent placeholder PDFs: if printing fails, History says why — out of paper, offline, wrong language and so on.

Private by default

Printing and editing work without internet. A connection is only needed for live data links, share service and update checks.
